# QingClaw IP logo candidates — 2026-11-14

English | [中文](README.zh.md)

Six independent square candidates generated from the three approved directions. These are concept masters only; no candidate has replaced production assets.

## Shared brief

- Subject: one extremely simplified, cute Qinglion cub.
- Brand ancestry: a large circular body entering from the left, one round negative-space opening on the right, and one broad folded plane at the bottom.
- Composition: upright, emerging from the lower-left, filling roughly 85–95% of the square.
- Complexity: 4–7 large shapes, two eyes, an optional tiny mouth, no outlines or decorative detail, readable at 32 × 32.
- Semantic colors: lion `#4BB6B0`, face and facial marks `#FFFFFF`, background `#48707A`.
- Treatment: ultra-clean rounded forms with barely perceptible neo-skeuomorphic depth.
- Constraints: no text, letters, wordmark, watermark, border, frame, scenery, extra subject, pointed mane, crown, photorealistic material, strong 3D rendering, or external cast shadow.
